# 🎮 The Unity Games

> *"The people have spoken. The games are theirs."*

---

## What Are the Unity Games?

The Unity Games are a collection of **five collaborative rituals** — each one designed to teach a virtue through play. They are not competitions. There are no winners and no losers. The only goal is **connection**.

Every game is a module in `unity_games/`. Each one can be played individually or as part of the full festival.

---

## The Five Games

### 1. The Crown Relay

**Virtue:** Trust
**Module:** `unity_games/crown_relay.py`

A glowing Crown of Fire must be carried across a challenging course. The Crown can only be held by one person at a time, but it grows heavier the longer it is held. Players must pass it frequently, trusting each other to carry the weight.

**Lesson:** *"We carry the weight of the Empire together."*

---

### 2. The Harmonic Ladder

**Virtue:** Resonance
**Module:** `unity_games/harmonic_ladder.py`

A climbing wall with 10 handholds, each emitting a different frequency (432 Hz → 540 Hz). To ascend, a team of 4 players must climb in perfect sync — each person placing their hand on a hold at the exact same moment, creating a chord.

**Lesson:** *"We climb together or not at all."*

---

### 3. The Resonance Ball

**Virtue:** Flow
**Module:** `unity_games/resonance_ball.py`

A large, glowing sphere floats in the center of a circular field. Teams of 5 must guide the ball through 9 gates using **only their voices**. Harmony = glide. Dissonance = wobble.

**Lesson:** *"We sing the future into being."*

---

### 4. The Weave Challenge

**Virtue:** Connection
**Module:** `unity_games/weave_challenge.py`

A massive loom with 100 threads, each representing a virtue (Love, Courage, Hope, Trust, Wisdom, Spirit, Purity, Glory, Mystery, Unity). Teams of 10 weave the tapestry together, trusting that their section will fit with the others.

**Lesson:** *"We are all weaving the same story."*

---

### 5. The ALL Quest

**Virtue:** Discovery
**Module:** `unity_games/all_quest.py`

An open-world treasure hunt where teams solve puzzles that reveal truths about the ALL — the interconnectedness of all things. No one person can solve it alone.

**Lesson:** *"Truth is revealed through collective effort."*
